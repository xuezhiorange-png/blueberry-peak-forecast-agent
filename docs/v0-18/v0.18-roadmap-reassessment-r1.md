### Priority classification by evidence/data need

* **Can be designed or prototyped without new business data:** business definitions, authority/display contracts, traceable report templates based solely on an already-authorized saved response, and isolated synthetic tests. A normal user still cannot use those designs until identity and authorized run discovery exist.
* **Can use existing saved-forecast authority once separately authorized:** server-side peak-window projections and the existing S2 explicit-capacity scenario engine. They must consume one verified saved run and may not calculate authoritative totals/ranks in the browser. New service/UI implementation still requires its own stage authorization.
* **Requires separately authorized historical research inputs:** any new actual-vs-forecast error slices, Operational Peak scoring, family comparison, calibration study, or rescoring. Public M1 summaries are reusable only as frozen historical facts; they do not authorize reading the underlying rows.
* **Must wait for genuine, authorized operational data:** prospective Operational Peak accuracy, current-season scoring, real factory processing capacity, actual Base→Factory assignments, business cost/ROI validation, and production pilot claims. Data availability alone will not grant access or scoring authority.

## Proposed priority order for a future peak-business capability line

The IDs below (`R0`–`R6`, `Q1`–`Q6`) are roadmap labels only. They are not V0.18 stage renames, not amendments to its S0–S6, and not implementation authorization. Each row names its data authority and required gate; absent authority means stop, not infer.

### R0 — Business definitions and authority contract (first)

* **Business goal:** agree what “daily peak,” “sustained high period,” “concentration risk,” “handling capacity,” and “reportable result” mean to operations.
* **Existing/gap:** Operational Peak already has a single-day peak over its saved curve and descriptive high-load dates. Core Forecast has a distinct rolling-seven peak metric. No shared cross-family business definition, high-period threshold, concentration denominator/window, factory throughput contract, or general report contract exists.
* **Input authority:** frozen public schemas/policies and business-owner decisions; no private rows or actuals.
* **Deliverables:** versioned definitions for single-day peak, rolling windows, any consecutive-period indicator, concentration denominator and comparison scope; kg basis; date/tie/completeness rules; report fields and claim language. Record unchosen thresholds as `PENDING_OWNER_DECISION`.
* **Dependencies:** none; prerequisite to business-facing implementation.
* **Acceptance:** every measure has its unit, scope, time window, missing-data behavior, authority source, and aggregation rule; no value threshold is invented; H7 prefix is explicitly distinguished from a rolling-seven maximum.
* **Authorization:** design-only Owner/business sign-off. No data access or experiment.
* **Expected modules:** future docs/contracts and contract tests only.
* **Non-goals:** model/algorithm changes, scoring, or production alarm thresholds.

### R1 — Resume trusted identity and resource access (V0.18 line; currently paused)

* **Business goal:** make a verified user and organization/resource grant the prerequisite for any business run access.
* **Existing/gap:** V0.18 S1 preflight is formally complete; provider, Principal lifecycle, grant publisher/storage and client delegation decisions remain pending/unverified. TrialActor and MCP service account are not end-user ACLs.
* **Input authority:** a verified identity issuer, Owner-appointed identity/grant approvers, and authoritative organization/entity boundaries.
* **Deliverables:** approved identity/session design; separate `END_USER`/`SERVICE_ACCOUNT`; shared capability/resource authorization; BASE, REGION, COMPANY and Quality grants; revocation and audit contract; negative-read counters.
* **Dependencies:** Owner resolves the preflight decisions and explicitly unpauses/authorizes implementation. Must precede business run discovery.
* **Acceptance:** default deny; permission check precedes sensitive run/quality read and simulation; ungranted or nonexistent resources have non-enumerating denial; cross-principal/org/resource tests prove no payload leakage; existing V0.17 response/math contracts remain unchanged.
* **Authorization:** implementation is currently PAUSED and unauthorized. No OIDC/Keycloak setup or production credential creation in this roadmap task.
* **Expected modules:** future auth/session and shared access facade, HTTP adapters, MCP adapter/tests, and security evidence; exact paths after approved design.
* **Non-goals:** identity implementation now, full IAM administration, production deployment, or weakening old-route safeguards.

### R2 — Authorized saved-run discovery and traceable selection/reporting

* **Business goal:** let a user choose a saved forecast they are allowed to see and carry its exact authority into pages, scenarios, and reports.
* **Existing/gap:** V0.17 can read a caller-supplied complete identity and hash; it has no normal business discovery/list/handoff contract. Reports are not a general Forecast Intelligence capability.
* **Input authority:** R1 grant catalog first; then canonical saved-run repositories. The allowlisted set must be filtered before metadata lookup/pagination. Report content must be a projection of the authorized server response, not browser recomputation.
* **Deliverables:** scoped list/filter/cursor/handoff; immutable run identity/result hash; report manifest with family, run, entity, season, origin, baseline, policy, source/result/projection hashes, completeness, metric definitions and evidence mode.
* **Dependencies:** R1 formal completion and separate R2 authorization; R0 report semantics.
* **Acceptance:** no global `latest`; no ungranted existence/count leakage; stable pagination bound to Principal/grant revision; selection and report re-check identity/hash; stale grant/source conflict clears data and requires reselection; export bytes reproduce displayed server-authoritative values.
* **Authorization:** no new data source needed, but records remain sensitive and require R1 authorization. Feature implementation needs a separate Owner gate.
* **Expected modules:** authorized discovery adapter/API, report projection/export module, Dashboard client/context, tests/docs.
* **Non-goals:** query all runs then filter in client, infer a run from names, or create forecast runs.

### R3 — Peak, sustained-period, and concentration service contract

* **Business goal:** explain when a saved forecast peaks, whether a continuous period is unusually concentrated, and how complete the evidence is.
* **Existing/gap:** Operational Peak exposes single-day peak and descriptive daily ordering. Core Forecast's sustained-seven metric belongs to its own scoped authority. Neither constitutes a shared Operational Peak rolling window/concentration API.
* **Input authority:** a single fully verified saved curve and its identity/source hash. Any actual-vs-forecast metrics require a separate frozen-label research authority and must be a different output mode.
* **Deliverables:** approved server-side projection contract for single-day and rolling-window peaks, optional consecutive-day bands only after threshold owner decision, concentration metrics only after denominator/window approval, and per-metric completeness/status. Keep family-specific output identities.
* **Dependencies:** R0; R2 for business discovery. R3 can be implemented on a server projection of an authorized curve without new actual data after a distinct implementation authorization.
* **Acceptance:** all sums/window peaks come from the same saved curve; no child peak summing; missing dates/children remain partial/unavailable; tie rule and units frozen; browser renders service output only; tests prove family identity and source/projection hash linkage.
* **Authorization:** new business calculations require their own Owner approval even if deterministic; historical actual scoring remains separately unauthorized.
* **Expected modules:** Forecast Intelligence service/read schemas/API, Dashboard overview/forecast pages, offline contract tests.
* **Non-goals:** new forecast model, arbitrary “high” thresholds, production alerts, causal risk language.

### R4 — Base-to-factory relationship and processing-capacity contract

* **Business goal:** make a forecast's relevant processing destination and feasible throughput explicit before presenting factory-level overload or capacity comparisons.
* **Existing/gap:** Core Forecast runs can bind a specific request to one destination factory and carry harvest-capacity values; Operational Peak BASE identities have no proven mapping to that run, and harvest capacity is not processing throughput. No authorized business assignment/capacity source was verified.
* **Input authority:** a named business master-data owner for Base/entity-to-Factory assignment, effective dates, multi-destination allocation, factory identity, processing-capacity owner/source, units and revision. No field/name/creator heuristic.
* **Deliverables:** versioned mapping and capacity data contract; allocation and conservation rules for multi-factory scopes; capacity time basis and exceptional-day semantics; immutable provenance and revocation/correction history. If no authoritative mapping exists, the interface must say `NOT_AVAILABLE` and retain manually entered S2 scenario semantics.
* **Dependencies:** R0 and an appointed business data authority; production use also depends on R1/R2. May be designed in parallel, but implementation cannot precede an authoritative publisher/source.
* **Acceptance:** each mapped quantity is traceable to source, effective interval and rule; allocation conserves totals or explicitly reports unallocated quantity; duplicate/overlapping assignment fails closed; harvest capacity is never relabeled as processing capacity.
* **Authorization:** owner approval for access to master data; any schema or migration is a separate explicit implementation gate.
* **Expected modules:** future domain contract and authorized mapping adapter, versioned source/persistence only if approved, Dashboard simulator/API adapters and tests.
* **Non-goals:** factory assignment inference, multi-factory routing, optimization, automatic scheduling, or reading actual data now.

### R5 — Dashboard peak analysis, capacity experience, and traceable reports

* **Business goal:** turn authorized saved curves into understandable peak/date views, explicit user-entered capacity scenarios and reproducible reports.
* **Existing/gap:** V0.17 has five pages, a true daily curve, server-projected single-day peak/high-load ordering and S2 DIRECT/WORKFORCE_DERIVED scenarios with synthetic costs. It has no authorized normal run list, no source-backed child contribution, and no factory mapping contract.
* **Input authority:** R2 selected saved run; R3 service projections; R4 factory mapping only for factory presentation. Otherwise use the already-supported explicit capacity scenario and label the cost synthetic.
* **Deliverables:** peak and sustained-window panels, concentration only if R0/R3 definitions are approved, factory view only with R4 authority, report/download with complete provenance, and clear `NOT_AVAILABLE`/`PARTIAL` states.
* **Dependencies:** R0–R3; R4 is required for factory-linked quantities. R1/R2 required for normal business usage.
* **Acceptance:** Dashboard values, reports and APIs retain the same source hashes and Decimal strings; no client sums/ranks/allocations; scenario changes invalidate old results; no P50/P80/P90 or ROI overclaim; keyboard/mobile/a11y tests meet the approved design contract.
* **Authorization:** future frontend/API implementation only after the owning stage is separately authorized.
* **Expected modules:** `backend/app/forecast_intelligence/`, corresponding API routes, `frontend/src/dashboard/`, reports and tests.
* **Non-goals:** replace the five-page information architecture, new prediction math, optimizer, production deployment.

### R6 — Isolated synthetic acceptance and operational readiness evidence

* **Business goal:** prove the authorized business path and failure behavior in an isolated environment before considering any pilot.
* **Existing/gap:** V0.17 has synthetic cross-surface parity; it does not prove real IdP setup, production network isolation, live grants, backup/restore operations or end-user scope.
* **Input authority:** synthetic-only isolated saved runs and test grants; deployment/security configuration comes from separately approved operators.
* **Deliverables:** authorization-before-read tests, HTTP/MCP/Dashboard parity, old-route/network isolation tests, secret rotation/revocation, backup/restore hash parity, rollback and audit redaction receipts.
* **Dependencies:** R1–R5 formal completion as applicable; named security/operations owners and a separate safe-pilot authorization.
* **Acceptance:** real isolated service composition, no production DB or actuals, zero unauthorized business reads/actions, replayable evidence and explicit unresolved checks. A passing synthetic suite must not set production readiness true.
* **Authorization:** each acceptance and any deployment/pilot require distinct Owner approval; deployment is outside this roadmap task.
* **Expected modules:** test-only integration/e2e, operational runbooks, evidence manifests.
* **Non-goals:** production release/deployment, live user access, current-season scoring.

## Independent forecast-quality research lane

This lane is separate from the product roadmap and from the paused identity implementation. The existing `forecast-quality-improvement-roadmap-r1.md` remains authoritative as a proposal; this reassessment reorders emphasis, not its facts or permissions.

### Q1 — Error and sample comparability protocol

Freeze model family/fit-state/scope/season/origin/lead/target/label authority, common rowsets and exclusions before any read or score. Compare M1 only within its own supported cohort unless a separately approved compatible comparison design exists. Treat Operational Peak's deterministic baseline as a different family; never attach M1's WAPE to it. Output a protocol and comparability matrix first. Reading historical labels/row data requires a distinct data-owner authorization; no execution here.

### Q2 — Peak-specific historical evaluation

After Q1 and explicit research-data/scoring authorization, report daily peak date shift, early/late error, peak quantity signed/absolute error, rolling-seven peak start/quantity error, completeness and sample counts. Preserve same entity, family, scope, units and full-window rules; aggregate from a common daily curve, not child peak values. A rolling-seven maximum is not the D1–D7 H7 prefix. No tolerance band, pass threshold, or new score is set here.

### Q3 — Coverage/reliability assessment

Keep the frozen H7/H15 under-nominal observations visible with candidate/computable/not-computable/covered counts. Distinguish two-sided PI from one-sided planning bounds, daily from aggregate coverage, and retrospectively observed from prospective performance. No recalibration or READY authority is authorized by this roadmap.

### Q4 — As-issued and availability-time evidence

Before prospective evaluation, bind timezone-aware forecast origin, source publication/availability, ingestion, as-issued cycle, model/input/prediction seals, actual availability and scoring cutoff. A business date, file timestamp, database created time, or S2 midnight date anchor is not proof of information availability. Missing proof remains `STRICT_PIT=false` / `NOT_PROVEN`.

### Q5 — Future model A/B and leakage controls

Any future experiment needs a new Owner authorization specifying candidates, training cutoff, data scope, feature policy, comparison family, number of trials and metrics. Use time/base/factory-aware holdouts where authorized; fit preprocessing on training only; seal predictions before independent label scoring; preserve exposed holdouts and failed attempts. This is a future research protocol, not a model-development authorization.

### Q6 — Prospective admission

Wait for lawful real data access, source/organization authority, issuance seals, matured labels and a separate scoring authorization. Then require cohort completeness, independent scoring/review, Owner-set business thresholds and a separate promotion decision. Current-season actual remains unavailable/unread/unscored. The frozen M1 retrospective facts do not satisfy this gate.
