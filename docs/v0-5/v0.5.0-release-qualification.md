## Frozen v0.5 capability

The prediction entity is `BASE`, with total productive area at base grain and an
inclusive April 15 business-season boundary. The active registry contains 39
bases; 38 are in the Yunnan core weather-comparable scope.

The delivered product path supports registered-base forecast, daily operational
forecast, the next complete 7-day and 15-day windows, their total kg / peak date /
peak kg, remaining-business-window forecast, immutable persisted runs, idempotent
create, canonical reload, REST, CLI, MCP stdio, and MCP Streamable HTTP.

The v0.5 MCP surface has nine tools total. The four operational peak tools are:

- `create_blueberry_operational_peak_forecast_run`
- `get_blueberry_operational_peak_forecast_run`
- `list_blueberry_operational_peak_forecast_runs`
- `get_blueberry_operational_peak_forecast_daily`

The existing five area-forecast tools remain part of the surface. See the [S5
operational evidence](s5/operational-7d-15d-peak-forecast-evidence-r1.json), the
[S6 product-path evidence](../next-version/evidence/operational-peak-product-path-s6.json),
and the [operational runbook](../runbooks/doubao-work-partner-mcp.md).

The selected operational policy is:

```ini
OPERATIONAL_PEAK_POLICY=OPERATIONAL_PEAK_POLICY_V1
SELECTED_BASELINE_ID=AREA_NORMALIZED_SEASON_WEEK_MEDIAN_V1
WEATHER_USED=false
```

W7 means `D+1...D+7`, W15 means `D+1...D+15`, and both require a complete
window. Ties use the earliest date. The remaining business window is a separate
output and must not be represented as a complete W7 or W15 window.

## Evidence and limits

S3 retained the simple business reference rather than the HGBR baseline. On
primary Fold B (39 validation bases, 25 seen and 14 natural out-of-base), the
reference daily/W7/W15 total WAPEs were `0.825783879425`, `0.679262173997`, and
`0.681542288125`; HGBR values were `0.838297828753`, `0.694795552375`, and
`0.682695349371`. This is evidence for retaining the reference, not accuracy
approval.

S4 was a matched lagged-weather ablation over 38 common bases. Weather improved
some W15 total and peak-quantity diagnostics, but did not provide a stable overall
gain: daily WAPE was `0.750618605576` versus control `0.750324886980`, W7 total
WAPE was `0.708109505437` versus `0.712491669120`, and W15 total WAPE was
`0.658896666944` versus `0.682719257474`. W7 peak-date error worsened. The
correct interpretation is `MIXED_WEATHER_EFFECT_NO_CLEAR_GAIN`; no weather-aware
operational model was selected.

## v0.4 relationship

v0.4.0 delivered the area-driven B1 path, farm-based prior yield, the historical
rolling-7-day metric, and five MCP tools. v0.5.0 adds the BASE entity and 39-base
registry, the April 15 business boundary, ERA5-Land historical research and its
ablation, operational next-W7/next-W15 semantics, the no-weather reference
baseline, four operational peak MCP tools, and the separate operational
authority/persistence/API/MCP path.

The v0.4 rolling-7-day peak metric is not the same metric as v0.5's next-W7
forecast window.
