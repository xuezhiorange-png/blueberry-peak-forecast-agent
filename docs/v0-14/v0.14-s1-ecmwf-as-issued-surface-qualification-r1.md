## Live GRIB temporal and unit semantics

Representative byte ranges at steps 3, 24, 144, 150, 168 and 360 from the accepted
00Z run were preserved and decoded with ecCodes 2.49.0. The public fixture binds
shortName, paramId, units, stepType/range, start/endStep, reference and validity
dates/times, and statistical/time-range keys where encoded.

| Parameter | Encoding | Meaning and current conversion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2t / 167 | K, instant, start=end=valid step | Instantaneous valid-time temperature; K−273.15 is correct, not a daily mean |
| 10u / 165, 10v / 166 | m/s, instant | Valid-time vector components; hypot(u,v) gives instantaneous speed |
| tp / 228 | m, accum, startStep=0 | Cumulative precipitation since this forecast start; ×1000 correctly yields mm |
| ssrd / 169 | J/m², accum, startStep=0 | Time-integrated downward solar energy, not instantaneous flux or net solar radiation |
| mn2t3 / 228027, mx2t3 / 228026 | K, min/max, 3h interval | Steps 3–144; last 3 hours |
| mn2t6 / 122, mx2t6 / 121 | K, min/max, 6h interval | 00/12 steps 150–360; last 6 hours |

The [ECMWF accumulation documentation](https://github.com/ecmwf/anemoi-datasets/blob/main/docs/building/sources/accumulate.rst)
distinguishes operational forecast-start accumulation from ERA5 step increments.
Live tp and ssrd encode ranges 0–24, 0–168 and 0–360, resolving this product's
actual accumulation origin. No cross-run difference is permitted.

The [ssrd parameter definition](https://codes.ecmwf.int/grib/param-db/169)
identifies downward solar radiation and the positive-downward convention.
Parameter 169 is not net solar radiation parameter 176, despite the product
table's loose “net … downwards” wording.

Candidate-only transforms are same-run cumulative differences, with ordered
steps, identical encoding and a separately predeclared nonnegative tolerance;
interval energy divided by interval seconds yields mean flux. S1 does not
freeze these as W1 features or choose a tolerance/interpolation policy.

All step-0 indices include min/max records whose own field step is 3. Those
records remain preserved but are not qualified as step-0 extrema. Their actual
field time, not the containing filename, governs eligibility.

## Asia/Shanghai and historical-proxy mapping

Shanghai midnight is 16:00 UTC on the previous date. Native 00/12 run steps
follow 3h/6h boundaries and do not supply that midnight boundary. A cumulative
interval crossing it cannot be partitioned exactly into local days without an
additional assumption. S1 authorizes no prorating, interpolation or carry-forward.

The committed [V0.7 weather contract](../v0-7/s2/weather-dataset-and-leakage-safe-feature-freeze.md)
describes 24 hourly samples per local day, sampled Tmin/Tmax, daily precipitation
and solar energy, and mean 10m speed. No private historical weather rows were opened.

All six full provider→daily-proxy mappings are PARTIAL, not frozen:

- Temperature: exact unit conversion, but instantaneous sparse samples are not
  the historical 24-hour local-day mean.
- Tmin/Tmax: true 3h/6h interval extrema are not hourly-sampled local-day extrema.
- Precipitation/solar: cumulative differencing and unit conversion are physical
  candidates; exact local-day allocation is not established.
- Wind: hypot is deterministic, but sampled instantaneous speed is not the
  historical local-day mean.

Run-relative windows remain a candidate for S2, not a policy decision. ERA5
realized weather never becomes a historical as-issued ECMWF forecast.
