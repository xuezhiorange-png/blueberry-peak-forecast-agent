## 7. S4 comparison and robustness conclusion

S4 used only the sealed S3 artifacts and preserved primary metric parity. Aggregate relative improvement was approximately 1.26% for H1, 3.39% for H7, and 3.45% for H15.

The final classification is:

`WEATHER_INCREMENTAL_VALUE=INCONCLUSIVE`

Reason:

`LEAVE_TOP2_ROBUSTNESS_REVERSES_OR_LOSES_IMPROVEMENT:H1`

For Combined H1, the original delta was `-0.0068421173759957838647451120`; after removing Top 1 it was `-0.0010525577361120777848942486`, but after removing Top 2 it became `+0.0047995251801890429250544798`. H7 and H15 remained improved after removing their Top 2 contributors, with deltas `-0.0067708840558050069958798238` and `-0.0052172714142282015235711299`.

Model B did not improve every Base. The improved actual-kg shares for H15 were `0.7471409909519334235321255744` in Fold A and `0.7073196327799868655009855839` in Fold B. These are evidence distributions, not business thresholds.

Therefore the conclusion is not that weather has no value. It is that H7/H15 show a more stable historical signal, while H1 remains sensitive to a small number of Base contributions; the evidence is insufficient for the `SUPPORTED_BY_HISTORICAL_OOT_EVIDENCE` classification.
