## Formula and cost authority

This is a deterministic asymmetric linear **loss contract**, not a new forecast
model, capacity planner or production economics service:

`underforecast_kg = max(actual_kg - forecast_kg, 0)`

`overforecast_kg = max(forecast_kg - actual_kg, 0)`

`BusinessLoss = C_under * underforecast_kg + C_over * overforecast_kg`

Every row exposes both quantities, both weighted losses and total loss. The
signed identity actual − forecast = under − over holds; under and over cannot
both be positive. Aggregate quantities/losses are sums of row terms, never
loss evaluated at an average forecast. Total equals under loss plus over loss.

BusinessCostContract is immutable and requires explicit ID, policy/version,
authority type/reference, both Decimal rates, loss unit and synthetic/canonical
flags. Allowed types are OWNER_BUSINESS_SUPPLIED and EXPLICIT_SYNTHETIC_SCENARIO.
There are no hidden rate/unit defaults. Missing/negative/non-finite/float inputs
are rejected. Canonical company cost is not established and true is rejected.
Owner-supplied schema mechanics do not manufacture a real cost authority.

Before historical result calculation, this R1 fixes exactly three contracts:

| Contract | C_under | C_over | Unit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| SYNTHETIC_BALANCED_R1 | 1 | 1 | SYNTHETIC_LOSS_UNIT |
| SYNTHETIC_UNDER_4X_R1 | 4 | 1 | SYNTHETIC_LOSS_UNIT |
| SYNTHETIC_OVER_4X_R1 | 1 | 4 | SYNTHETIC_LOSS_UNIT |

All use EXPLICIT_SYNTHETIC_SCENARIO, synthetic=true and
canonical_company_cost=false. The 4:1 ratio is a predeclared sensitivity
demonstration, not a company shortage/overage-cost estimate. Synthetic units
are neither CNY nor USD. Rates are never adjusted after seeing results.

CANONICAL_COMPANY_COST_ESTABLISHED=false; REAL_ROI_VALIDATED=false.
Labor, cold-storage, packaging, transport, quality-loss and emergency-capacity
costs are future-only and not implemented. A future Owner/business authority
may supply real explicit rates under separate governance.

## Fair comparison and horizon completeness

H1/H3/H7/H15 are prefixes D1…DH, as in S4. The comparison universe for each H
contains all admitted historical origins and its expected prefix targets.
Only **complete common origin-prefixes** with actual and all three candidates
available for every D1…DH enter that H's comparison. This rule is frozen before
execution. It is deliberately stricter than using isolated computable daily
rows: one missing/non-computable planning day excludes that origin-prefix for
all three candidates. No missing actual or planning value is zero-filled.

Each cost contract independently evaluates all candidates on the same common
set. Point loss is recomputed on that set, not compared using its larger full
rowset total. Evidence reports expected/source/computable/actual/common/excluded
counts, complete scorable-origin counts, rowset hashes and candidate source and
payload hashes. Exclusions include entire prefixes required by completeness.
If no complete common prefix exists, comparison is
NOT_COMPARABLE_INCOMPLETE_ROWSET with no candidate loss comparison.

Comparison gates require equal cost-contract hash, actual authority/value hash,
comparison rowset, target semantics and horizon. Changed source/payload under
the same candidate identity is rejected. Comparisons across cost scenarios are
not a legal forecast comparison. No winner, ranking, automatic model selection
or production planning change is issued. loss_delta_vs_point is only a signed
**synthetic loss-unit difference**, never savings, profit, revenue or ROI.

V0_16_EXPLICIT_COST_AUTHORITY_R1 and V0_16_SAME_COST_CONTRACT_COMPARISON_R1.

values have canonical strings independent of presentation trailing zeros.

REPLAY and PUBLISH phases. Operator-local root arguments must not be published.

labels and frozen predictions; unchanged S2 replay must pass. REPLAY runs in a
fresh process and distinct output root. PUBLISH requires identical contracts,
private input bytes, summaries, receipts and manifest hashes before projecting
public evidence. Process receipts are separate from deterministic artifacts.

losses, scenario/candidate names, statuses, policy and hashes only: no Base IDs,
