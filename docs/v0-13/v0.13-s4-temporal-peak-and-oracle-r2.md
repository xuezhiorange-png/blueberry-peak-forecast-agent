## Temporal authority and definitions

Complete authoritative H15 same-origin views: Fold A 1043, Fold B 2921,
Combined 3964. All four S3 H15 WAPE values replay exactly after the label gate.
These are local 15-day windows, not season-wide peak authority.

Peak semantics reuse `research_records.score_curve`: earliest single-day maximum
and earliest maximum complete seven-calendar-day sum. No cross-origin stitching.
Date/quantity MAE is pooled by window count, not averaged across fold MAEs.

For shape, Q=sum(actual), P=sum(predicted), E=sum(abs(predicted*Q/P-actual)).
The full-authority pooled shape requires every required window to have Q>0 and
P>0. Zero-mass windows are reported, not filled with epsilon or selectively
removed to publish a favorable subset score. Under the frozen zero-shape-mass
rule, an incomplete required shape forces timing INCONCLUSIVE, including when
both combined date errors degrade. Partial numerator/denominator fields are
diagnostic only and must not be converted to a supported full-cohort shape score.

## Frozen timing gates and interpretation

WEATHER_TIMING, GDD_TIMING and COMBINED_TIMING are all **INCONCLUSIVE** under the
complete frozen gate; no named family is SUPPORTED. All three have worse combined
single-day and rolling7 date MAEs than M0, and none improves both dates in both
folds. Required pooled shape is NOT_COMPUTABLE, so this must not be relabeled
NOT_SUPPORTED by dropping zero-mass windows.

Weather-only reduces peak quantity errors but increases combined single-day date
MAE by 0.073158 days and rolling7 start-date MAE by 0.096872 days.
GDD-only increases these date MAEs by 0.246216 and 0.097376 days.
Weather+GDD increases them by 0.112765 and 0.148083 days.
Amplitude improvement is not timing support. Different families cannot supply
different halves of one timing claim.

Oracle versus M1 reduces combined single-day date MAE by 0.775479 days and
rolling7 start-date MAE by 0.047679 days. Single-day timing improves in both folds,
but rolling7 timing degrades in Fold A by 0.376798 days and improves in Fold B by
0.199247 days. Combined shape is NOT_COMPUTABLE; no claim of synchronous shape
improvement is established. Versus M0, Oracle improves the combined single-day
date by 0.702321 days but worsens rolling7 start date by 0.049193 days.

Thus extra future-weather visibility has mixed local timing evidence, not a
reproducible two-fold solution for both dates. If both dates improved in every
fold it would indicate timing information missing from past-observed features;
if neither improved it would not demonstrate a timing solution. The actual
accepted R2 result is mixed, not either stronger hypothetical claim.
Oracle classification is exclusively
`NOT_APPLICABLE_RESEARCH_UPPER_BOUND_ONLY`; it cannot enter Lane A ranking or
candidate promotion.
