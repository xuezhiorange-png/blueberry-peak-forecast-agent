- All 675 publisher XML polygons are semi-auto. 100 selected candidate boxes have no documented independent human confirmation. No qualified real GT exists: Precision/Recall/F1/AP/MAE/Bias and COCO metrics are NOT_EVALUABLE/null.
- Candidate classes remain unapproved; color is not a maturity grade. Counts are visible candidates per image, never kg.
- Public references are EXPOSED_PUBLIC_BENCHMARK; research seals provide integrity logs, not independently enforced access control. Offline IDs do not authenticate humans.

- Capture time, base, variety, lighting and occlusion are unknown where not supplied. Cross-base/date/variety holdouts are UNVERIFIED, not fabricated.
